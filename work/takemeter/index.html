TakeMeter

Sorts NBA comments into analysis, hot take or reaction.

Nov 2026In progress

takemeter.txtNovember 2026page 001

What it is

TakeMeter is a text classifier I built for CodePath’s AI201 course. It reads a comment from the NBA community on Lemmy (!nba@lemmy.world) and sorts it into analysis, hot take or reaction. I went with Lemmy because Reddit blocked scripted reads.

How it works

I pulled the 1,494 most recent comments through Lemmy’s public API and filtered out what wasn’t one person’s take, like 615 box score bot posts, links, and very short or very long comments. That left 710 to read. The hardest boundary was analysis versus hot take, so I wrote a rule for it: cover the fact with your thumb. If the point still stands without it, it’s a hot take.

The labels were made with AI, and a note on every row says how. Claude Code labelled all 283, and Gemini labelled 263 of them separately as a check. They agreed on 209 (79%). After re-reading the 54 disagreements against the rules, 11 labels changed. Then I fine-tuned DistilBERT on a 70/15/15 split with the default settings, on a laptop CPU.

Where it stands

Unit 5 is done. The first run got 0.628 accuracy and 0.458 macro F1. It missed all 8 analysis posts in the test split and called 7 of them hot takes. Unit 6 is next: a baseline comparison, three seeds, and checking my labels against the staff set.
