Unofficial Guide

Answers campus questions from 88 student guides.

Oct 2026Done

unofficial-guide.txtNovember 2026page 001

What it is

The Unofficial Guide answers questions about campus life from 88 short student-written guides: course workloads, housing reviews, dining tips, and the advice official sites leave out. I built it for CodePath’s AI201 course. Answers come only from the documents and cite the files they used.

How it works

It’s a retrieval pipeline. The guides are split into 800 character chunks, embedded with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and stored in Chroma. For each question it pulls the five closest chunks. A relevance gate refuses the question if even the best chunk is too far off, so off-topic questions, like ones about diesel engines, don’t get made-up answers. Otherwise Gemini writes the answer from those chunks.

I wrote five criteria and an eval script that runs the test questions three times with caching off.

Where it stands

Done for the course. The first eval missed on citations. A question about Aldridge Hall got refused even though the right file was retrieved, because I had set the cutoff from distances that didn’t match the real system. Raising the cutoff from 0.6 to 0.7 fixed it, and off-topic questions were still refused. Two things are still broken. A dining question ranks buildings with “Hall” in their names above the right answer. And the first question after startup took 5.24 seconds, over my 4 second target.
