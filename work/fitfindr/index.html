FitFindr

Shopping agent for secondhand clothes.

Nov 2026Done

fitfindr.txtNovember 2026page 001

What it is

FitFindr is a shopping agent for secondhand clothes, built for CodePath’s AI201 course. You type what you want in plain words, like “a vintage graphic tee under $30, size M”. It searches 40 listings from Depop, thredUp and Poshmark, picks the best match, suggests one or two outfits using pieces from your wardrobe, and writes a short caption you could post.

How it works

A planning loop runs one step at a time: parse, search, select, suggest, card. Price and size come out of the query with regex, not a model. The search tool filters on size and price, ranks by keyword hits, and runs on an MCP server. If nothing matches, the loop stops before the outfit step. It re-runs the search without the price, then without the size, to tell you which part of your request to change. Gemini writes the outfit and the caption. Every run can be traced step by step.

I wrote five pass/fail criteria first, then a script that scores every try by the same rules. The eval runs 10 scenarios five times each.

Where it stands

Done for the course. The first eval passed all five criteria, but a closer look showed 36 of 45 captions opened with “Scored this” or a close variant. I counted that as a miss, changed the caption prompt, and re-ran. The criterion passed, but the template had just moved to “Channeling…” openers, and I wrote that up rather than call it fixed.
