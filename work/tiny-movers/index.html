Tiny Movers

Co-op Roblox experience: mice hauling loot out of a house at night.

Oct 2026Building

tiny-movers.txtNovember 2026page 001

What it is

Tiny Movers is a co-op Roblox experience I’m building. You and your crew are mice, and every night the house is your job site. Before each night, the Big Cheese, a fat boss rat, deals five jobs in the burrow. Finish three to pass. Fall short and the crew takes a strike, and three strikes get you kicked out.

How it works

The house is about 20 times avatar scale, so a kitchen counter is a climb. Jobs mix stealing and chaos: smash two eggs, climb the fridge, boop the sleeping cat, ride the roomba. A noise meter fills when you sprint on hard floors, fall or break things, and the cat reacts to it. It patrols, stalks and pounces, then carries its catch back to its bed, where a crewmate can untangle them. Fragile loot like an egg shatters into three pieces for good after one hard knock.

Movement got the most work. I started from my own Godot controller (acceleration windup, head bob, landing kick) and built on it: wide carving turns, skids, a dive into a slide, a double jump flip, and a climb where you hang, wind up a meter and lunge. The props and characters are modelled in Blender and baked over invisible box colliders, with painted pixel textures.

Where it stands

It’s still in progress. The kitchen, hallway, living room, bathroom and bedroom are built, and they open one by one over the first four nights. The garage is partly built. My last logged test run passed 114 of 114 Luau tests.
