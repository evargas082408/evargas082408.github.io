Frame 01/04

Nest One

An indoor flying robot for people who find stairs and long walks hard. Ask for your keys from the hall shelf and it flies them to your chair, lands, and opens its bay. I co-founded Nested Robotics to build it. My part right now is the downwash: ducts and a closed-loop toroidal prop, run through OpenFOAM, so it doesn't blow things off the table it lands beside.

  • V1 airframe: CAD
  • Toroidal prop: CFD

SINEW-1

A humanoid right arm and hand. Eighteen tendon "muscle" modules sit in the forearm, five actuators move the arm, a Meta Quest 3 drives it, and a DIY glove pushes force back into your fingers. The goal is to record hand tracking from the Quest and train a model on it to move like a person. The CAD, the boards and a simulator exist. The arm doesn't, yet.

18 tendon modules · 7-DOF arm · 16 actuated hand joints · 12 boards, clean ERC and DRC

Oct 2026Designed, not built

Crow

An alarm clock you can't snooze, unplug or uninstall your way out of: a battery-backed ESP32 with a phone app that holds the only key, over Web Bluetooth. A simulator runs the firmware next to the real app, and the FreeCAD enclosure fails its build if any parts overlap. Nothing is printed or flashed yet.

About $29 in parts · 208 automated checks

2026Simulated

VC2 core regulator

A two-phase buck converter that steps 12 V down to 1.0 V at 20 A, a small version of the regulators that feed a GPU. A Python script works out every part value from the spec, and I checked the loop and the switching in ngspice.

3.0 mVpp ripple · 74° phase margin · About 27 mV on a 5 to 15 A step

2026Planned

Tessera

A small tensor-core-style matrix engine in SystemVerilog: 4x4 INT8 tiles, INT32 accumulation, one tile per clock, checked bit for bit against NumPy. I want to feel why GPUs moved matrix math into dedicated blocks. The plan is written; the RTL isn't started.

4x4 INT8 tiles · INT32 accumulation

Tiny Movers

A co-op Roblox experience where you and your friends are mice hauling loot out of a house at night. A fat boss rat hands out jobs, a cat and a roomba get in the way, and anything fragile shatters if you drop it. Most of my time went into movement: wide carving turns, skids, and climbing you have to time.

5 rooms built · 114 of 114 Luau tests passing · House at about 20x avatar scale

Night Shift

A pixel-art action RPG where you're the night clerk at a corner store in a city that runs on an old program. Every night at 2:00 AM glitches crawl out of the walls. You fight them in real time, then freeze time and queue up patches, written in real Python in Code Mode or crafted from fragments in Story Mode.

Godot 4.7 · Real-time combat · Real Python 3.13 · Chapter 1 in progress

Wildbound

A survival overhaul for Minecraft Bedrock, written in TypeScript. Version 0.1.0 brought thirst, body heat, four seasons, 23 creatures and six bosses. Since then it has grown realms, villages and castles, a crafting table, a food chain with great beasts, a Terraria-style chain of world Ages, and a combat system where every weapon has its own moves. It builds and passes its offline checks; most of it still needs real testing inside Minecraft.

v0.11.0 · 94 entities · 34 weapon models · 636 offline combat checks
